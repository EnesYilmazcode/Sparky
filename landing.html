Your 3D circuit workbench

Put resistors, LEDs and a 9V battery on a virtual breadboard, wire them up, and run the circuit. When an LED stays dark, the AI tutor reads your board and tells you why.

Free, in your browser. The demo needs no account.

Place, wire, simulate, learn.

The same steps as a real breadboard, minus the burnt LEDs.

  1. 1

    Place parts

    Drop parts onto a 700-hole breadboard with power rails along the top and bottom. Orbit and zoom to see exactly where each lead lands.

    • Resistor
    • LED
    • 9V battery
    • Buzzer
    • Push button
  2. 2

    Wire it up

    Click a hole or a pin, then click another, and a jumper wire drops in between. Pick from six wire colors.

  3. 3

    Simulate

    Press play and the solver works out every node voltage and current with Modified Nodal Analysis. It flags backwards LEDs and shorts, and when a part runs over its current rating it tells you which resistor to add.

  4. 4

    Ask the tutor

    The AI tutor reads your parts and wires, so it can explain what your circuit is doing or build one for you. Its changes show up as a preview you accept or decline, and every build is checked in the simulator first.
